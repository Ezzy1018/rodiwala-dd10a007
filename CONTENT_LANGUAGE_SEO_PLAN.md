# Rodiwala content language and SEO transition plan

## TLDR

Rodiwala should move from formal, English-only supplier copy to an English-led Hinglish system.

- Keep URLs, page intent, primary product terms, titles, canonicals, structured data and internal information architecture stable.
- Rewrite the visible customer-facing copy in natural Roman-script Hinglish, with English construction terms retained where people actually use them.
- Do not translate every sentence or create duplicate English and Hinglish pages yet.
- Pilot the change on the homepage, `/buy-construction-material` and `/contact`, then roll it through catalogue, material and support pages after checking qualified WhatsApp enquiries.
- Use real site-team language from contractors, supervisors, builders and homeowners. Do not manufacture slang or repeat keywords unnaturally.

The final user note appears to mean that Hinglish is more suited, because the preceding argument supports Hinglish. This plan follows that interpretation.

## 1. The strategic decision

The site currently explains the business clearly, but it sounds like a polished English supplier brochure. That is adequate for comprehension and machine-readable SEO. It is weaker at the moment that matters commercially: making a local buyer feel that the business understands site work and is easy to contact.

The right move is not “English versus Hindi”. It is a three-layer language model:

| Layer | Language rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Search and page intent | English product and category terms, supported by natural Hindi/Roman Hindi variants | Preserves discoverability for searches such as `construction material supplier`, `10 mm rodi`, `M-sand`, `RMC` and `buy construction material` |
| Brand and persuasion | Natural Hinglish in Roman script | Feels familiar to Delhi NCR and North Indian site audiences using WhatsApp and mixed-language speech |
| Technical and trust content | Plain English with Hinglish explanation where useful | Avoids ambiguity around grades, quantities, freight, access and suitability |

This gives the business a local voice without making the site look like a forced translation or a keyword page.

## 2. What should change, and what should not

### Change

- Hero headlines, supporting copy, CTA labels and microcopy.
- Section headings and explanatory paragraphs.
- WhatsApp prefilled messages.
- FAQ questions and answers, where the answer remains factually accurate.
- Material descriptions, use-case language and enquiry checklists.
- Internal link anchor text, so it sounds useful rather than generic.
- Image alt text where the current text is generic, while keeping the material name explicit.
- `public/llms.txt` wording so machine-readable descriptions reflect the same positioning.

### Preserve

- All existing URLs, including `/materials`, `/buy-construction-material`, `/aggregate/10-mm-rodi`, `/aggregate/20-mm-rodi`, `/stone-dust`, `/m-sand`, `/ready-mix-concrete`, `/delivery-areas`, `/how-it-works`, `/about` and `/contact`.
- One clear search intent per indexable page.
- Primary English category and material terms in the title, H1 or opening text where they describe the page.
- Canonical URLs, robots directives, sitemap coverage and server-rendered content.
- Evidence-safe delivery language. The site can accept enquiries from across India, but it must not turn that into a blanket delivery promise.
- Technical boundaries. Do not let conversational copy prescribe structural mixes, grades or suitability that should be confirmed by a contractor or engineer.
- The quote-led model. Rodiwala is not a fixed-price cart, lender or universal stock catalogue.

## 3. Recommended brand voice

### Voice position

Rodiwala should sound like a reliable person who understands a site requirement and helps close the material gap quickly.

The voice is:

- Direct, practical and local.
- Helpful without pretending to be the engineer.
- Familiar without becoming jokey or over-familiar.
- Confident about the process, careful about claims.
- Short enough for a phone screen and WhatsApp conversation.

### Language rules

- Use Roman-script Hinglish first. Do not switch to Devanagari in the first release.
- Keep product names in their familiar market form: `10 mm rodi`, `20 mm rodi`, `stone dust`, `M-sand`, `crushed sand`, `RMC`, `TMT steel`, `bricks` and `blocks`.
- Use common words such as `samaan`, `site`, `quantity`, `location`, `rate`, `delivery`, `truck`, `unloading`, `grade` and `requirement`.
- Prefer `mangwao`, `bhejo`, `bata denge`, `check karenge` and `site tak` over formal Hindi or unnatural literal translations.
- Keep numbers, units and specifications exact. `20 mm` must not become an approximate local phrase.
- Avoid fake local colour such as `bhai`, `jugaad`, `sabse sasta`, `pakka`, `turant` or `har jagah delivery` unless the owner explicitly approves those claims and can support them.
- Use one language consistently within a block. A short English term inside a Hinglish sentence is fine. A sentence that changes language every few words is tiring.

### The proposed line

Do not use `Construction material chahiye, khadiye yahan se`.

Use one of these instead:

1. `Construction material chahiye? Rodiwala se mangwao.`
2. `Construction ka samaan chahiye? Yahin se requirement bhejo.`
3. `Rodi, M-sand ya RMC chahiye? Site details bhejo, quote le lo.`

The first is the strongest short brand line. The second is broader and more conversational. The third is useful when the hero needs product relevance as well as a CTA.

## 4. Content architecture for every page

Every important page should follow this order:

1. **Immediate answer:** Say what Rodiwala supplies or helps the visitor do in the first screenful.
2. **Local recognition:** Use the words a contractor, supervisor or homeowner would use when asking for the material.
3. **Specific requirement:** Explain the information needed to quote, such as material, size or grade, quantity, location, date and access.
4. **Operational clarity:** Explain what gets checked and what is confirmed.
5. **Proof boundary:** State what is not promised when that protects trust.
6. **Action:** Give one primary WhatsApp action and one secondary call action.
7. **Related intent:** Link to a relevant material, buying page or delivery explanation.

This structure is better for users and search because it makes the page useful even if the visitor has never heard of Rodiwala. It also prevents Hinglish from becoming decorative copy with no information value.

## 5. Page-by-page content direction

### Homepage `/`

**Role:** Brand discovery, category relevance and first WhatsApp enquiry.

**Keep in SEO fields:** `construction material supplier`, `India`, `rodi`, `stone dust`, `M-sand`, `ready-mix concrete` and the Rodiwala brand.

**Suggested visible direction:**

- Eyebrow: `Rodi, M-sand aur RMC ke liye seedha quote`
- H1: `Construction material chahiye? Rodiwala se mangwao.`
- Intro: `10 mm aur 20 mm rodi, stone dust, M-sand ya RMC ke liye apni quantity, site location aur required date WhatsApp par bhejo. Availability, freight aur delivered rate check karke batayenge.`
- Primary CTA: `WhatsApp par quote lo`
- Secondary CTA: `Call karke poochho`
- Supporting proof: `Requirement bhejo. Site ke hisaab se quote milega.`

Do not remove the English category phrase entirely. The H1 can be Hinglish, while the eyebrow, opening paragraph, page title and supporting headings carry the English search context naturally.

### Buy page `/buy-construction-material`

**Role:** Capture high-intent commercial searches and explain the quote process.

**Suggested visible direction:**

- H1: `Construction material khareedna hai? Pehle requirement bhejo.`
- Opening answer: `Rodiwala par fixed-price cart nahi hai. Material, quantity, delivery location aur date bhejo. Team availability, route, freight aur site access check karke delivered quote confirm karegi.`
- Checklist heading: `Quote ke liye ye details bhejo`
- Steps: `Material batao`, `Quantity aur site bhejo`, `Delivered rate confirm karo`, `Dispatch ka time fix karo`
- Trust section: `Site ke hisaab se quote, guesswork nahi.`

The page title should remain close to `Buy Construction Material in India | Rodiwala`. The visible H1 can be more human because Google may use both the title tag and prominent page text when forming a result title.

### Catalogue `/materials`

**Role:** Help visitors recognise the material they need and move to a specific quote.

**Suggested visible direction:**

- H1: `Site ke liye kaunsa material chahiye? Yahan se dekho.`
- Intro: `Rodi, stone dust, M-sand aur RMC ke options dekho. Jo material chahiye, uski quantity, location aur date bhejkar delivered quote lo.`
- Card labels: `10 mm rodi`, `20 mm rodi`, `Stone dust`, `M-sand`, `RMC`
- Card CTA: `Iska quote lo`
- Card detail link: `Details dekho`
- Secondary requirement: `Cement, TMT, bricks ya koi aur samaan chahiye? Requirement bhejo.`

Keep each card description materially different and useful. Do not use the same Hinglish sentence across every tile.

### Material detail pages

**Role:** Capture material-specific searches and convert a visitor who already knows what they want.

**Template:**

- H1 keeps the exact material name, for example `20 mm Rodi for your construction site` or `20 mm Rodi chahiye? Requirement bhejo.`
- First paragraph gives the English term and the common local term: `20 mm aggregate, jise site par 20 mm rodi bhi bola jata hai...`
- Uses section: `Ye material aam taur par kahan use hota hai`
- Checklist: `Quote ke liye kya bhejna hai`
- Boundary: `Final suitability apne contractor ya engineer se confirm karo.`
- CTA: `20 mm rodi ka quote lo`

The material data model should eventually support a short English description, a Hinglish description, local terms, applications and quote note separately. That will prevent one paragraph from doing too many jobs.

### Aggregate page `/aggregate`

**Role:** Help the visitor distinguish 10 mm and 20 mm without giving unsafe technical advice.

Suggested H1: `10 mm ya 20 mm rodi? Jo specification hai, wahi bhejo.`

Keep the comparison table. Rewrite its supporting copy in simple Hinglish, but retain `coarse aggregate`, `concrete-related work`, `dense reinforcement areas` and other technical terms where they are necessary.

### Delivery page `/delivery-areas`

**Role:** Reduce uncertainty about whether a route and site can be served.

Suggested H1: `Aapki site tak delivery possible hai? Location bhejo.`

Use `city`, `locality`, `pin code`, `route`, `vehicle access` and `unloading` in both English and natural mixed-language sentences. Keep the current caveat that feasibility is checked case by case. Do not publish Delhi NCR, Noida, Ghaziabad or UP landing pages until the business confirms actual service coverage and can support each page with useful local information.

### How it works `/how-it-works`

Suggested H1: `Requirement bhejo, hum rate aur delivery check karenge.`

The four steps should feel like a real WhatsApp exchange, not a generic SaaS process:

1. `Material aur quantity bhejo`
2. `Site aur date batao`
3. `Availability aur delivered rate check hota hai`
4. `Dispatch ka time confirm hota hai`

### About `/about`

Suggested H1: `Rodiwala site ke material ko simple banata hai.`

Explain who the service helps: `thekedar`, `builders`, `site engineers`, `site supervisors` and `homeowners`. Do not imply a large team, own fleet, owned stockyard or years of experience unless the owner confirms those facts.

### Contact `/contact`

Suggested H1: `Site par kya chahiye? WhatsApp par requirement bhejo.`

Use a prefilled message that mirrors real speech while retaining structured fields:

`Namaste Rodiwala, meri site ke liye material chahiye. Material: [naam]. Quantity: [quantity aur unit]. Location/pin code: [location]. Required date: [date]. Site access ya unloading note: [details]. Availability aur delivered rate bata dijiye.`

The form-like fields are valuable because they make the first message complete. The greeting and sentence around them make it feel human.

## 6. SEO protection rules

### URLs and canonicals

Do not change existing URLs during the language transition. If a URL ever must change, publish a permanent redirect, update internal links and sitemap entries, and verify the old and new URLs before launch.

Keep one canonical page per intent. Do not create `/hi/`, `/hinglish/` or city pages simply to repeat the same copy.

If a fully translated Hindi version is added later, it should be a genuinely useful alternate page with its own URL and reciprocal `hreflang` implementation. Hinglish in Roman script is not a reason to invent a new locale or claim a separate language version.

### Titles, H1s and descriptions

Keep the primary English search phrase in the title or opening content where it accurately describes the page. Example:

`Buy Construction Material in India | Rodiwala`

The visible H1 can be:

`Construction material khareedna hai? Pehle requirement bhejo.`

Do not place all spelling variants in titles or headings. `Rodiwala`, `Rodi Wala` and `Roadi Wala` can be handled naturally in one relevant explanatory sentence or FAQ if there is a genuine user reason. Repeating variants is keyword stuffing and weakens the brand.

Write a unique description for every page. The description should pitch the page in the language of the customer, while retaining the exact product or category terms needed for clarity.

### Visible copy and rendering

Make all important copy real text in the server-rendered DOM. Do not hide SEO text in CSS, images or a client-only interaction. The current SSR setup should be preserved and rechecked after content refactoring.

### Structured data

Keep structured data aligned with what is visibly present on the page. If FAQ answers become Hinglish, the FAQ schema answers must use the same answer, not an English-only hidden variant. Treat schema as clarity for machines, not as a ranking shortcut.

### Content quality

Every new paragraph must answer a real buyer question or help complete an enquiry. Do not add Hinglish filler to increase word count. Do not publish a page about a locality, price, stock level, certification, review or delivery guarantee without approved evidence.

## 7. Rollout plan

### Phase 0: Baseline and content inventory

Before changing copy:

- Export current titles, descriptions, H1s, canonical URLs, indexability and sitemap URLs.
- Record the last 28 days of Search Console impressions, clicks, queries, CTR and average position for each indexable URL.
- Record WhatsApp clicks, call clicks and quote CTA clicks by page.
- Capture the current homepage, buy page, catalogue, one material page and contact page on desktop and mobile.
- Build a phrase bank from real WhatsApp messages, phone calls and three to five target users. Ask what they call each material and what they would type into Google.
- Identify unapproved claims before the rewrite. Remove or flag them instead of making them sound more persuasive.

### Phase 1: Pilot the highest-value pages

Rewrite only:

- `/`
- `/buy-construction-material`
- `/contact`

Keep URLs and core SEO metadata stable. Change visible language, CTA labels, WhatsApp messages and page-specific copy. Keep the primary English terms in opening content and metadata.

Run for at least 14 days, preferably against comparable 28-day windows. Judge the pilot on qualified enquiries and message completeness, not just clicks.

### Phase 2: Roll through commercial and product pages

Rewrite:

- `/materials`
- `/aggregate`
- `/aggregate/10-mm-rodi`
- `/aggregate/20-mm-rodi`
- `/stone-dust`
- `/m-sand`
- `/ready-mix-concrete`

Validate that each page has a distinct purpose, unique description, exact material terminology and at least one relevant internal link.

### Phase 3: Rewrite trust and operational pages

Rewrite:

- `/how-it-works`
- `/delivery-areas`
- `/about`

Use a slightly calmer register here. These pages need to reassure procurement and engineering-minded visitors, not only sound local.

### Phase 4: Build the supporting search layer

Only after the core pages are stable, publish useful guides from the existing content brief queue, such as:

- `10 mm vs 20 mm aggregate: how to choose`
- `What is rodi aggregate?`
- `M-sand vs river sand: what to check before ordering`
- `How to request a construction material quote`
- `What affects delivered construction material pricing?`
- `Construction material delivery checklist for site teams`

Each guide should answer the search question in the first paragraph, include local terminology naturally, link to the relevant product page and end with a complete WhatsApp enquiry path.

## 8. Measurement and decision rules

### Primary business measures

- Qualified WhatsApp enquiries per 100 organic sessions.
- Percentage of enquiries containing material, quantity, location and date.
- Quote requests that receive a usable follow-up.
- Call clicks and WhatsApp clicks by landing page.
- Enquiries that progress to a confirmed quote or order, once that tracking is available.

### SEO measures

- Indexed pages and sitemap errors.
- Impressions and clicks by page and query cluster.
- CTR for branded, material-specific and commercial queries.
- Average position for `construction material supplier`, `buy construction material`, `rodi supplier`, `10 mm rodi`, `20 mm rodi`, `stone dust supplier`, `M-sand supplier` and `RMC supplier`.
- Query language mix, including Roman Hindi and English variants.

### Quality checks

Test the pilot with:

- One contractor or thekedar.
- One site supervisor or engineer.
- One homeowner who has recently arranged material.

Ask each person:

1. What do you think Rodiwala does?
2. What material do you think you can ask for?
3. What would you send on WhatsApp?
4. What would make you hesitate?

### When to investigate

Do not react to a ranking or traffic movement in the first few days after a rewrite. Google may recrawl and reprocess title and page changes over days or weeks. Compare like-for-like windows and annotate every release.

Investigate if, after a reasonable recrawl period:

- A page loses impressions for its core intent while the page remains indexed and technically healthy.
- CTR drops without a matching improvement in qualified enquiries.
- Users click WhatsApp but send incomplete messages.
- Search queries become broader but less relevant.
- The page starts attracting a location or product query that the business cannot actually serve.

If this happens, first review the page title, opening answer, internal anchors and search intent. Do not immediately revert all Hinglish copy. The issue may be weak targeting or an unclear offer rather than the language itself.

## 9. Content production workflow

Use one content source of truth before editing route files. For each page, maintain:

- Page URL and search intent.
- Primary query and secondary query cluster.
- Approved title and meta description.
- H1 and first-screen copy.
- Hinglish terms and English technical terms.
- Facts that require owner confirmation.
- Primary CTA and WhatsApp context.
- Internal links in and out.
- Schema text, if used.
- Reviewer and approval date.

Because the current site is a static React application, centralise repeated copy such as CTA labels, checklist language, process steps and WhatsApp templates in content modules. Keep page-specific SEO metadata next to the route or in a typed page-content object. This reduces accidental mismatch between visible copy, schema and metadata.

## 10. Final recommendation

Adopt Hinglish for connection, not as a blanket translation layer. The strongest version of Rodiwala is:

`English for what the page is about. Hinglish for why a local buyer should trust and contact it. Plain technical language for what must be exact.`

The current SEO work should be treated as the foundation, not discarded. The main opportunity is to make the first screen and the WhatsApp journey sound like the people who actually order construction material. Start with the three-page pilot, use real user language, preserve the URL and metadata structure, and let qualified enquiry data decide how far the Hinglish register should go.
