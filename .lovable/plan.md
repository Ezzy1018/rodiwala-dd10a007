# Rodiwala visual and content redesign

## Direction
- Use the supplied RW roof mark as the primary brand device in the header, footer, page intros, graphic watermarks, and favicon without over-repeating the full wordmark.
- Keep the approved forest, lime, off-white, Space Grotesk, and DM Sans system; increase contrast, scale, imagery, and editorial composition.
- Follow the references structurally: an immersive photographic homepage intro with the offer and quote action, an image-led catalogue with actions placed on the imagery, and a visual step-by-step process.

## Homepage
- Replace the split intro with a deep-green image panel that integrates the headline, supporting copy, contact actions, and the supplied logo mark.
- Rebuild the material catalogue as larger photographic tiles with image overlays and clear “Get a quote” actions.
- Turn “How it works” into a richer mixed-size visual process composition rather than four plain cards.
- Retain the working quote builder, delivery content, FAQs, and evidence-safe wording while improving their visual hierarchy.

## Internal pages
- Give every internal page a branded, image-led introduction instead of the shared plain text banner.
- Expand material detail pages with distinct uses, enquiry checklist, related materials, and a strong quote section.
- Deepen Materials, Delivery Areas, How It Works, About, Contact, and Request Quote with content specific to each visitor task; no thin copies of homepage sections.
- Preserve every existing URL and navigation path.

## Technical details
- Add the extracted transparent RW logo asset and derive the favicon from it.
- Rework shared page and catalogue components so the richer system stays consistent across routes.
- Keep all colors, typography, corners, overlays, and shadows in the global design tokens.
- Verify the homepage and representative internal pages at desktop and mobile sizes, including menu and quote actions.
